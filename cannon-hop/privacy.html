Cannon Hop · Privacy

Updated November 7, 2026.

Cannon Hop is an offline puzzle game with no account, advertising, tracking, analytics, or third-party service SDK. The game does not automatically collect or send personal information and requests no device permissions.

Progress and settings are saved locally on your device. Replaying a stage keeps your completed-stage progress. Settings has no progress-erasure action. Deleting the app removes its local data from this device; copies may remain in system device backups. There is no cross-device sync.

If you choose to email player support, your email provider processes the message you send, and the developer receives the details you voluntarily include to answer your request. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Player support and privacy questions: wuhoodev@gmail.com.

反冲小炮 · 隐私说明

更新于2026年11月7日。

本游戏离线运行，无账号、广告、追踪、分析或第三方服务SDK。游戏不会自动收集或发送个人信息，也不请求设备权限。

进度与设置保存在设备应用存储内，重玩关卡会保留已完成记录。设置中没有清除进度操作。删除 App 可移除此设备上的应用本地数据，系统设备备份中可能仍保留副本。没有跨设备同步。

如果你主动邮件联系玩家支持，邮箱服务会处理你选择发送的邮件，开发者会为回复请求而收到你自愿提供的内容。请避免发送不必要的个人信息。
