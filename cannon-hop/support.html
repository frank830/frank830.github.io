How to play

The direction buttons form one row, ordered left to right as ↑ ↓ ← → (Up, Down, Left, Right). Choose a direction, then press the separate large Fire button below the row. A shot hits the first target. If space permits, recoil slides the cannon backward until a blocker, remaining target, or edge stops it. Empty shots can reposition you. The selected direction follows the barrel and stays selected after a shot. Change it whenever you need.

Clear all targets within your ammo. Undo restores a move and its bullet. Hint suggests the next direction to try. The original six stages are freely selectable; extensions unlock in order. Progress records completed stages, without an in-game star score.

The gear button opens Settings directly, with controls for sound effects, haptics, reduced motion, and language. The stage selector shows one chapter at a time and completion counts. To replay a stage, select it again; when out of ammo, use Retry. Replaying keeps your completed-stage progress.

Sound and saves

The game plays sound effects only, with an in-game switch. There is no background music or music setting. Effects respect Silent Mode and stop in the background or during an interruption. Progress and settings are saved on this device; there is no account or cross-device sync. There is no in-game progress-erasure action. Deleting the app removes its local data from this device; system backups may still retain a copy.

方向按钮保持一排，从左到右严格为 ↑ ↓ ← →。先选方向，再按下方独立的大开火按钮。子弹打中第一个靶子；空间允许时，小炮反向滑到障碍、仍在的靶子或边界之前。空发也能换位。默认选中炮口当前朝向，开火后会保留该方向，可随时换向。

用有限子弹打完全部靶子。撤销恢复上一步和子弹；提示只建议下一次尝试的方向。原六关自由选择，扩展关卡逐关解锁。关卡只记录是否完成，不显示游戏内星级评分。

齿轮按钮直接进入设置，可调整音效、触觉、减少动态效果和语言。选关页每次显示一章及完成计数；需要重玩时可重新选择该关，弹药耗尽时可按重开。重玩会保留已完成记录。
