PageFit · Privacy policy

中文Support

Your images stay under your control

PageFit processes the image you choose on your device and creates a PDF locally. The app has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer-operated server. It does not upload your images, PDFs or usage activity to the developer.

The system Photos and Files pickers let you choose the image to import. PageFit does not scan your whole photo library. A file stored with a cloud provider may be downloaded by that provider when you select it.

Local data and sharing

Exported PDFs are stored in the app's local Documents/Exports folder and are visible through Files. Paper and language preferences are stored locally. The app does not use these preferences for tracking.

Files leave the app when you choose a share destination or save a copy elsewhere. Those destinations, including cloud drives and messaging apps, operate under their own privacy policies. Device backups are controlled by your system settings.

You can delete exported files in Files. Deleting the app removes its local app data, but not separately saved or shared copies, or copies retained in system backups.

If you contact support

If you email wuhoodev@gmail.com, the developer receives the email address and any message or attachments you choose to send and uses them to respond to your request. Do not send private images or PDFs unless they are necessary, and remove sensitive details first.

This support website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information such as your IP address and browser details to operate the site. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. These pages contain no added analytics or advertising scripts.

整页 · 隐私说明

整页在设备上处理你选择的图片，并在本地生成 PDF。App 没有账号系统、广告 SDK、分析 SDK 或开发者运营的服务器，不会把图片、PDF 或使用活动上传给开发者。

图片和文件

你通过系统照片或文件选择器指定要导入的图片。整页不会扫描整个相册。如果选中的文件来自云盘，系统或相应服务商可能需要先下载该文件。

导出的 PDF 保存在应用的 Documents/Exports 文件夹，可从「文件」访问。纸张和语言偏好保存在本地，不用于跟踪。

只有在你选择分享对象或另存位置时，文件才会交给相应 App 或服务。云盘、通讯工具等接收方适用各自的隐私政策。设备备份由系统设置控制。

删除数据

可以在「文件」里删除导出的 PDF。删除 App 会移除设备上的应用本地数据，不会删除另存或分享出去的副本，系统备份中也可能保留数据。

联系支持

当你向 wuhoodev@gmail.com 发邮件时，开发者会收到你的邮箱地址，以及你主动发送的正文和附件，用于处理这次咨询。请勿发送不必要的私人图片或 PDF，发送前请移除敏感信息。

本页面由 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 可能为运行网站处理 IP 地址、浏览器等请求信息，具体见 GitHub 隐私声明。本页面没有额外加入分析或广告脚本。
