PageFit · Support

Long image to multi-page PDF.

中文Privacy policy

Make your PDF

  1. Choose one image from Photos or Files, or try the included fictional sample.
  2. Choose A4 or US Letter. Check the suggested page breaks, then drag a break when you want a different cut. Use undo to restore a previous adjustment.
  3. Export and inspect the real PDF. Scroll or zoom to check the result, then save or share it using the system share sheet.

Where are the files?

Exports are saved in Files → On My iPhone → PageFit → Exports. You can save another copy to a location you choose in the share sheet. The original image is not changed.

Delete unwanted exports in Files. Deleting PageFit removes its local app data, but does not remove copies you saved or shared elsewhere. System backups may retain data.

Before you print or share

Check every page break. Suggestions look for lighter areas, not paragraph meaning. PDF pages contain images, without OCR or editable text. The app does not sharpen blurry images, stitch screenshots or capture other apps.

Use PNG, JPEG or HEIC. If an image cannot be opened, try a smaller copy or export it as PNG or JPEG. For a PDF that looks blurry, start from a clearer original. Select the matching paper size in your printer settings.

整页 · 使用帮助

选一张已有长图，调整分页线，再导出多页 PDF。

怎么用

从照片或文件中导入一张图片，也可以体验内置虚构示例。选择 A4 或 Letter 纸张，检查分页线的位置，需要时拖动调整，或撤销上一步。导出后，在实际 PDF 预览里逐页检查，再用系统分享面板保存或分享。

文件保存在哪里

打开「文件」→「我的 iPhone」→ PageFit → Exports，可以找到导出的 PDF。分享时也可以另存到自己选择的位置。原始图片不会被修改。

不需要的导出文件可在「文件」里删除。删除 App 会移除设备上的应用本地数据，另存或分享出去的副本不受影响，系统备份中也可能保留数据。

导出前再检查一下

自动分页只参考浅色区域，不理解段落。PDF 内容是图片，没有 OCR 可搜索文字或可编辑正文。整页不拼接截图，不跨 App 截图，也不会把模糊的原图变清晰。

支持 PNG、JPEG 和 HEIC。如果图片无法打开，可以改用较小的副本，或转存为 PNG、JPEG。打印时，请把打印机的纸张大小设为与导出时相同。
