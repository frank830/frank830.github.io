Quick help

The essentials, at a glance.

PurrFit is designed to be simple and calm. These four tips solve most questions.

↔

Move & rotate

Drag a piece onto the white cells. Tap a piece to rotate it before finding its perfect spot.

✓

Exact fit

Every open cell must be covered exactly once. Pieces cannot overlap or extend outside the board.

FAQ

Common questions.

Tap a question to see its answer.

How do I complete a level?

Fill every white cell with the available pieces. A level clears only when every open cell is covered exactly once, with no overlaps and no part of a piece outside the board.

Why won’t a piece stay where I dropped it?

The placement is not valid yet. Check that the piece is fully inside the open area and does not overlap another piece or a blocked cell. Try rotating it or placing it in another spot.

What does Exact Hint do?

Exact Hint shows a correct location for one remaining piece. Depending on the hint available, you can use the shown placement as guidance or choose Place It to position that piece automatically.

Why am I being offered an ad for a hint?

After the included hints have been used, PurrFit may offer an optional rewarded ad for another hint. You never need to watch an ad to continue playing or to solve a level yourself.

Where is my progress stored?

Your completed levels and unlocked progress are saved locally on your device. Deleting the app may remove that progress, and it does not currently sync between devices.

How can I update my privacy choices?

When privacy options are required in your region, open PurrFit and use Privacy Choices on the start screen. If the form does not load, check your internet connection and try again.

The game is not working correctly. What should I try?

First close and reopen PurrFit, then confirm that your device is running the latest available app version. If the problem continues, restart your device and send a support request with your device model, iOS version, and the steps that caused the issue.

Still stuck?

Tell us what happened.

Open a support request and include your PurrFit version, device model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem. Screenshots are welcome when they help explain the issue.
